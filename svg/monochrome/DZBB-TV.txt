DZBB-TV (VHF Channel 7 Analog, UHF Channel 15 Digital) is a television station in Metro Manila, Philippines, serving as the flagship of the GMA television network. The digital service is known as DZBB-DTV by only a few people. It is owned and operated by the network's namesake corporate parent alongside GTV flagship DWDB-TV (channel 27). Its studios are located at the GMA Network Center, EDSA corner Timog Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, while DZBB-TV's hybrid analog and digital transmitting facilities are located at the GMA Tower of Power, Tandang Sora Avenue, Barangay Culiat, Quezon City, with single-frequency network (SFN) relay towers located at the PBCom Tower in Makati, Zen Towers in Manila, Antipolo, Angeles City, Cabanatuan, Malolos, and Jalajala.
